Good to know

A few honest limits.
Works best in Chrome or Edge on Android and desktop, and Safari on iPhone. Accuracy depends on your mic and the room, so speak at a normal pace and expect to fix a word or two. Your browser sends the audio to its own speech service to transcribe it, so avoid reading out passwords or anything sensitive.

Your transcript stays on this device until you copy or save it.
